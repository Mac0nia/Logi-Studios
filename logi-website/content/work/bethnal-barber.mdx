The biggest shift wasn't aesthetic — it was operational. Before the site, roughly 80% of customers were walk-ins. That meant unpredictable revenue and no way to plan staffing. Three months after launch, 65% of bookings come through the website.

The online booking system also changed the kind of customers they were getting. New customers now arrive having already chosen their barber and knowing the price. Fewer no-shows, fewer conversations about cost.

Revenue per week is up roughly 30%. The site paid for itself in the first fortnight.
